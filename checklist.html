CUT & KEEP

El Niño ready checklist

Tick these off at home. Print it and stick it on the fridge.

  • 1 Follow official updates. Get El Niño news from PAGASA and your local government, not forwarded posts.

  • 2 Save water early. Fix leaks, reuse rinse water for plants, and store clean water in covered containers.

  • 3 Beat the heat. Drink water often, avoid strenuous work at midday, and check on older people, babies and anyone sick.

  • 4 Plan the planting. Farmers: ask your municipal agriculture office about planting dates and drought-tolerant varieties.

  • 5 Prevent fires. No open burning of trash or fields during dry spells; clear dry grass around homes.
