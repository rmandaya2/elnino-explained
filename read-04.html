Get ready, not worried.

El Niño is announced months ahead, which gives households, farms and towns time to prepare. Five habits make the biggest difference:

  1. Follow official updates. Get El Niño news from PAGASA and your local government, not from forwarded posts.
  2. Save water early. Fix leaks, reuse rinse water for plants, and store clean water in covered containers so mosquitoes can't breed in it.
  3. Beat the heat. Drink water often, avoid strenuous work at midday, and check on older people, babies and anyone who is sick.
  4. Plan the planting. Farmers can ask their municipal agriculture office about planting dates and drought-tolerant varieties before the dry months hit.
  5. Prevent fires. Avoid open burning of trash or fields during dry spells, and keep dry grass cleared around homes.
